# Xinghe Intelligence (Fictional Company) Employee Handbook, 2026 Edition

This handbook is a fictional document for the RAG demo. All company names, people and numbers are made up, so you can check that answers really come from the retrieved text and not from the model's general knowledge. It is an English translation of xinghe_handbook.md.

## 1. About the Company

Xinghe Intelligence was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Binjiang District, Hangzhou. Its main business is visual quality-inspection systems for small and medium-sized manufacturers, and its core product is called "Eagle Eye QC". As of June 2026 the company has 312 employees, 58% of whom work in R&D. It has branch offices in Shenzhen and Chengdu: the Shenzhen office handles hardware, and the Chengdu office handles customer success and after-sales support.

The company's mission is "Let every production line see its defects." The founder and CEO is Lin Zhiyuan, and the CTO is Zhou Ruoxi.

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance

The company uses flexible working hours. Core hours are 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and employees must be at work during core hours. Total working time is at least 8 hours a day (including a 1-hour lunch break).

Every Wednesday is a "no-meeting day". No meetings are scheduled except for handling production incidents.

Employees may work remotely up to 6 days a month. Requests must be submitted in the "Xinghe OA" system one working day in advance and approved by the direct manager. Employees on probation may work remotely up to 2 days a month.

Arriving more than 30 minutes late without prior notice counts as half a day of personal leave.

## 3. Leave

Annual leave: employees who have worked for one full year get 10 days of paid annual leave, plus 1 day for each additional year, up to 20 days. Unused annual leave can be carried over until March 31 of the following year, after which it expires.

Sick leave: 5 days of fully paid sick leave per year. Days beyond 5 are paid at 80% of base salary and require a hospital certificate.

Birthday leave: employees get 1 extra day of paid leave in their birthday month.

Marriage and maternity leave follow national and Zhejiang provincial regulations. In addition, the company provides 5 extra days of paternity leave (up to 20 days in total).

## 4. Expenses

Business travel lodging: up to 650 yuan per night in tier-1 cities (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen) and up to 450 yuan per night in other cities.

Meal allowance: 120 yuan per day while traveling, paid as a flat daily amount with no receipts needed.

Transportation: use the subway and buses for local travel where possible. After working overtime past 9 p.m., employees may take a taxi home and claim the actual cost, linked to that day's overtime record in "Xinghe OA".

Deadline: all expenses must be submitted within 60 days; claims after 60 days are not accepted. A single claim over 5,000 yuan needs approval from both the department head and the finance director.

## 5. Training and Growth

Each employee has a learning fund of 3,000 yuan per year for books, online courses or tech conferences. Spending beyond 3,000 yuan requires an application reviewed by the department head.

Every other Friday at 4 p.m. the company holds "Xinghe Night School", an internal talk series. Employees sign up with their own topics, and each speaker receives a 300-yuan award.

New employees are assigned a "navigator" (mentor) and have at least one one-on-one per week during their first three months.

## 6. Performance and Promotion

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in January and July. Ratings are S, A, B and C, with no more than 10% rated S.

Employees rated S twice in a row, or rated A or above three times in a row, may apply for a promotion review. Promotion reviews are held every April and October.

The year-end bonus is usually paid in February of the following year. The baseline is 2 months' salary, adjusted by the individual performance factor (S=1.8, A=1.3, B=1.0, C=0).

## 7. Information Security

All employees must use company-issued computers to handle customer data. Uploading customers' production-line images to any external cloud drive or public AI service is prohibited.

Work computers must have full-disk encryption enabled and lock after no more than 5 minutes. Suspected data leaks must be reported to the security team within 1 hour via security@xinghe.example or internal extension 8110.

## 8. FAQ

Q: What should I bring on my first day?
A: Your original ID card, original degree certificates, a bank card (China Merchants Bank preferred), and the separation certificate from your previous employer.

Q: Who do I contact when IT equipment breaks?
A: Submit an IT ticket in "Xinghe OA", or go to the IT service desk on the 3rd floor, open 9:30 to 18:30 on working days.

Q: Is there a company shuttle?
A: Yes. In the morning, shuttles leave from Exit B of Binhe Road subway station at 8:30 and 9:00; in the evening they leave from the company's main gate at 18:30 and 20:30.
